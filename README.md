# HarmonEye

Real-time music harmony analysis and visualization.

> Ever wondered what are all the notes and chords in the music you like? I did, but I'm no Mozart with absolute pitch. Instead I have created HarmonEye!
> 
> What looks like a retina of an eye is a unique software application that allows you to see what you hear!
> 
> With all the deep math inside it analyzes the music that your computer plays to the speakers or hears via a microphone and visualizes it. From any song. In real-time.
> 
> The intensity of each tone in an octave is shown in a segment of a circle (like on the clock). A loud tone makes a long red segment. From that you can easily see the melody, chords and the key of your favourite songs. Including the nuances like a singer's vibrato.
> 
> You can write it down. Or just look at it for hours and be amazed like me!

It detects pitch classes (tones without octave) in audio and shows them in a circle.

This repository contains a library, macOS and Android applications and some experiments
(using Welcome to [Processing](https://processing.org):
- `harmoneye-android`
- `harmoneye-commons`
- `harmoneye-desktop`
- `harmoneye-experiments`

Originally built in 2014. Not maintained since. The domain expired,
the Android app was removed from Play Store, the macOS app was only for Intel Mac.
